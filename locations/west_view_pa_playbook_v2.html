Property Management — West View / North Hills Guide
✅ SELF-MANAGE: VIABLE
West View's working professional and family tenant base — driven by North Hills SD quality and Route 19 commuter access — produces manageable self-management conditions. Near-balanced owner/renter split (~52/48%) means lower turnover frequency than majority-renter markets. Self-managing is viable if you're north corridor local and within 30 minutes. Best option: a leasing-only PM arrangement for tenant placement, then self-manage the ongoing relationship. Model the full 10% PM fee in your long-term hold regardless.
🏢 PM: SOLID OPTION — ESPECIALLY AT SCALE
Multiple PM firms serve the north hills corridor (West View, Ross Township, McCandless, Bellevue). Coverage here is solid and competition keeps fee structures reasonable. Full PM is the right call at 2+ properties or if you're not local to the Route 19 corridor. Ask specifically about North Hills SD tenant pipeline and days-to-lease in 15229 — north hills professional tenants lease faster than south/east markets at comparable rents.
