DORMONT VS. BEECHVIEW — SAME ZIP, DIFFERENT TAX STRUCTURE
Dormont Borough (40.8442 mills) and Beechview / City of Pittsburgh (27.307 mills) share ZIP code 15216 but are different municipalities with different tax structures. Dormont's combined rate is nearly 50% higher than the Pittsburgh city rate applied to Beechview. Always confirm the parcel is in Dormont Borough and apply this model — not the Beechview model. The calculator uses CLR 50.14% × 40.8442 mills.

Property Management — Dormont / South Hills Borough Guide
⚠️ SELF-MANAGE: DUPLEX ONLY
The playbook recommends triplex and quad properties in Dormont — and unit count is where self-management gets genuinely harder. Managing 3–4 units means more lease events, more maintenance coordination, and more tenant relationships to juggle simultaneously. Self-managing a duplex in Dormont is viable for a local investor. Self-managing a triplex or quad here is workable but requires real availability and a proven local contractor network. Be honest about your bandwidth before committing to self-managing 3+ units in any market.
✅ PM: RECOMMENDED FOR TRIPLEX / QUAD
For Dormont's 3–4 unit strategy the PM fee compounds well — each additional unit spreads the management cost and per-unit overhead drops. A PM with south hills light rail corridor experience leases faster than most self-managers, reducing the vacancy drag that matters most given Dormont's current rent-softness (−9% YoY flag). PM is the right default for 3+ units here; revisit for a duplex if you're south-side local with the bandwidth for it.
