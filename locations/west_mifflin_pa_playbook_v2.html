WEST MIFFLIN IS A HIGH-MILL MARKET — CONFIRMED 45.24 COMBINED MILLS
West Mifflin Borough is served by West Mifflin Area School District (West Mifflin, Whitaker) at 27.2771 mills — a heavy school levy. Combined with the borough (11.53) and county (6.43), the total confirmed rate is 45.2371 mills — the 4th-highest in the playbook, roughly 10 mills above Bridgeville (34.87) and 7 above Bethel Park (37.89). Taxes are the single biggest constraint on cash flow here. The calculator uses the 2026 CLR of 50.14% × 45.2371 mills. Pull the actual parcel tax bill before every offer — at this mill rate, a stale-high assessment does real damage to NOI.

💡 TAX APPEAL — HIGH-VALUE HERE, FILE AGGRESSIVELY
Because West Mifflin's combined mill rate is so high (45.24), a successful assessment appeal saves more per dollar than almost anywhere in the playbook. If you acquire above the CLR-modeled value — or inherit a stale-high assessment — a BPAAR appeal can save $600–$1,200/yr, materially moving the deal. File before the annual deadline (typically late March / September 1 for the following year). At this mill rate, always model the appeal scenario alongside the base case.

West Mifflin is the solid-income, high-tax market in the playbook. Household incomes (~$74,600) and rent growth (+10% YoY 2BR) are among the healthier in the Mon Valley band, and the borough is stable and ~78% owner-occupied — but the 45.24-mill combined rate and a thin ~22% renter pool make cash flow the binding constraint, not demand. It's an income-quality and stability play with genuine tax drag, not a raw-yield play. File the tax appeal on every deal.

Property Management — West Mifflin / Mon Valley Guide
✅ SELF-MANAGE: VIABLE IF LOCAL
West Mifflin is a reasonable self-manage candidate. Employed service and commuter tenants, a stable owner-heavy borough, and modest turnover mean you're not fighting the tape on tenant quality. The catch is the thin renter pool — leasing takes longer than in eastside markets, so tenant placement is the skill that matters most. If you're local to the South Hills / Mon Valley corridor, self-managing a West Mifflin duplex or triplex is workable. Best option: a leasing-only PM arrangement — pay one placement fee for screening and lease-up, then self-manage the ongoing relationship.
🏢 PM: GOOD OPTION IF SCALING OR REMOTE
Full PM is a solid choice if you own multiple properties in the Mon Valley / South Hills corridor or aren't reliably within 30 minutes. Firms active in 15122 have vendor networks and leasing pipelines. But with the tightest cash-flow margins in the playbook (high taxes) and a thin renter pool, the 10% fee bites — ask specifically about days-to-lease and whether they charge while a unit sits vacant. The leasing-only arrangement captures the most valuable PM service (screening and placement) without the ongoing monthly cost.
