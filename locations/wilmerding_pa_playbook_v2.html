🚨 THE WILMERDING RULE: WIDEN THE RADIUS, VERIFY THE INCOME
Wilmerding's fundamentals are strong — 66% renters, a walkable Westinghouse grid, taxes below the Mon Valley benchmark — but only two confirmed in-borough duplex sales trade in a year. You cannot build a pipeline on Wilmerding alone, so treat Turtle Creek, Pitcairn, North Versailles, and East McKeesport as one market with it. And because the real MF comps ($120K–$155K) sit above where a conservatively-underwritten duplex clears financing, the deal only works one of three ways: buy below ~$115K, add unit count (triplex/quad), or verify in-place rents above the conservative underwrite. Skip that verification and the low-price thesis evaporates on the first turnover.

Property Management — Wilmerding / East Allegheny Corridor Guide
🚫 SELF-MANAGE: NOT RECOMMENDED FOR MOST INVESTORS
Wilmerding has a demanding management environment for a market this small. The ACHA/HCV inspection process requires a PM with established relationships who knows the process cold — a first-time self-manager navigating an ACHA inspection adds 30–60 days of vacancy. Low renter income (~$37K median household, 29.8% poverty) means more payment follow-up and more turnover. Self-managing here is viable only for highly local investors with prior ACHA experience and a proven East Allegheny-corridor contractor network. For most investors, self-management here is how you lose the yield advantage the low acquisition price created.
✅ PM: STRONGLY RECOMMENDED — THIS IS THE INVESTMENT
In Wilmerding the PM is not overhead — it is the investment thesis. A PM with active ACHA/HCV experience in 15148 (or the adjacent Turtle Creek/Pitcairn ZIPs) turns the low renter income profile from a risk into a stable income source: direct-deposit HAP payments, established inspection timelines, and eviction process experience. The 10% fee is cheap relative to the cost of mishandling one ACHA inspection delay or one eviction without proper process. Confirm ACHA experience and active unit count in the corridor before hiring anyone here.
