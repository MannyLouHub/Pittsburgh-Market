Property Management — McKeesport / Mon Valley Guide
🚫 SELF-MANAGE: NOT RECOMMENDED FOR MOST INVESTORS
McKeesport has the most complex management environment in the playbook. The ACHA/HCV inspection process requires a PM with established relationships who knows the process cold — a first-time self-manager navigating an ACHA inspection adds 30–60 days of vacancy. Higher eviction frequency means legal process familiarity matters. Low renter income (~$22K median) means more payment follow-up and more turnover. Self-managing here is viable only for highly local investors with prior ACHA experience and a proven Mon Valley contractor network. For most investors, self-management here is how you lose the yield advantage the low acquisition price created.
✅ PM: STRONGLY RECOMMENDED — THIS IS THE INVESTMENT
In McKeesport the PM is not overhead — it is the investment thesis. A PM with active ACHA/HCV experience in 15132 turns the low renter income profile from a risk into a stable income source: direct-deposit HAP payments, established inspection timelines, and eviction process experience. The 10% fee is cheap relative to the cost of mishandling one ACHA inspection delay or one eviction without proper process. Confirm ACHA experience and active unit count in 15132 before hiring anyone here.
