Why Highland Park for Your Strategy
✅

✅

💡 TAX APPEAL — WORTH FILING AFTER CLOSE

Property Management — Highland Park / East End Guide
✅ SELF-MANAGE: STRONGLY VIABLE
Highland Park is the strongest self-manage candidate in the playbook. Professional tenants — UPMC, CMU, tech sector — are income-qualified, low-drama, and tend to renew leases. Turnover is infrequent. Maintenance calls are reasonable. The 10% PM fee has its highest opportunity cost here: on a $285K duplex it runs ~$2,730/yr and directly compresses already-moderate margins. If you're within 30 minutes and can respond to a maintenance call within a day, self-managing is the right call at Highland Park prices. Best option: a leasing-only PM arrangement — pay for tenant screening and placement (one-time fee), then self-manage the ongoing relationship.
🏢 PM: BEST AT SCALE OR IF REMOTE
Full PM makes sense once you own 3+ properties and your time has real opportunity cost, or if you aren't local to the East End. At Highland Park's price points the monthly PM fee is the highest in the playbook set — make sure you're getting real value for it. A leasing-only arrangement at $1,250–$1,450/mo placement cost is a strong middle option: you get professional tenant selection without the ongoing monthly overhead.

✅ STRONG PM COVERAGE IN THE EAST END
The East End is one of the most actively managed residential corridors in Pittsburgh. Multiple PM firms have active portfolios in Highland Park, East Liberty, and adjacent neighborhoods. Competition among PMs here is higher than suburban markets — use it to negotiate fee structures and verify performance.
MODEL PM AS PERMANENT
At Highland Park's price points, the difference between PM-managed and self-managed returns is approximately $2,700/yr on a duplex. Model the full 10% PM fee in your long-term hold analysis even if you plan to self-manage in the short term. PM exit should always be in your underwriting.
