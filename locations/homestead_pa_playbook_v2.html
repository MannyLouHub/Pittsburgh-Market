Property Management — Homestead / Mon Valley Guide
🚫 SELF-MANAGE: NOT RECOMMENDED FOR MOST INVESTORS
Homestead shares the management complexity profile of McKeesport. ACHA/HCV inspection process requires a PM with established Mon Valley relationships. Higher eviction frequency, lower renter income (~$24K median), and block-level vacancy risk all require experience that most part-time self-managers don't have. Self-managing here without prior Mon Valley and ACHA experience is the fastest way to erase the yield advantage the low acquisition price created. If you have McKeesport management experience that includes ACHA, self-management may be viable — otherwise, PM from day one.
✅ PM: STRONGLY RECOMMENDED — ACHA IS THE STRATEGY
The PM is not overhead in Homestead — the PM IS the investment thesis. ACHA/HCV payment standards that exceed market rents, direct-deposit income, and lower effective vacancy with experienced management turn Homestead's demographic profile from a risk into a structural income advantage. Require confirmation of active ACHA units in 15120 or adjacent Mon Valley ZIPs (15122 W. Homestead, 15120 Munhall) before executing any PM agreement. Days-to-ACHA-inspection-pass is the most important performance metric to ask about.
