⚙️ HOW ALLEGHENY COUNTY ASSESSMENT WORKS — CRITICAL TO UNDERSTAND
Base year is 2012. The Common Level Ratio (CLR) is 52.7% for 2025 — your property is assessed at ~52.7% of market value, not the purchase price. This means your tax bill is significantly lower than you'd expect at full value. A $200K purchase has taxes calculated on ~$105,400. Total combined millage = 43.5382 mills.

⚠️ THE PM REALITY CHECK — READ THIS BEFORE EVERY OFFER
A PM company at 10% of collected rents is a permanent expense that must be included in every scenario. This isn't optional for you — it's a business model decision. That means your minimum cap rate at purchase needs to be 8%+ (not 6–7%) to still generate meaningful cash flow after PM, taxes, insurance, and debt service. Deals that "work" for a self-managing investor often don't pencil for a PM-managed investor at the same price. Always run the PM-adjusted numbers.

Purchase Price Tier Guide — PM-Adjusted, Crafton 2–4 Units
HOW TO READ THIS — All scenarios assume: 20% down · 7.75% rate · 10% PM · 8% vacancy · Duplex $1,750 GPR / Triplex $2,510 GPR
TIER 1 — SWEET SPOT
$100,000 – $149,000
🟢 STRONG BUY (if cosmetic-only scope)
This is the only duplex price band with meaningful room to absorb a permanent 10% PM fee under conservative rents. Cosmetic-only opportunities at this basis will be unusual; run the analyzer with verified taxes, utilities, condition, and rent roll before treating a listing as a buy.
TIER 2 — CORE TARGET RANGE
$150,000 – $185,000
🟡 BUY IF NUMBERS WORK
At this price band, duplex margins are highly sensitive to actual rents and expenses once 10% management is included. Prefer legal triplex opportunities or documented rent upside, and require the analyzer to clear your PM-adjusted thresholds before offering.
TIER 3 — TRIPLEX / QUADPLEX TERRITORY
$185,000 – $240,000
🟡 DUPLEXES DON'T PENCIL — 3–4 UNITS ONLY
A standard duplex in this range is unlikely to tolerate 10% PM under conservative rents. Require three or four legal units, verified rent support, and a positive analyzer result; do not rely on projected lease-up alone to rescue the deal.
TIER 4 — HIGH CAUTION
$240,000 – $300,000
🔴 ONLY 4-PLEX WITH STRONG RENTS
Only a legal four-unit property with strong documented income belongs in this band under the 10% PM model. Approaching $300K requires unusually strong rents or value-add potential that may exceed the cosmetic-only strategy; treat unsupported projections as a pass.

💡 PM FEE UNDERWRITING — MODEL IT AS A PERMANENT EXPENSE
Never underwrite a deal assuming you'll eventually self-manage. If you're building a PM-managed portfolio under Luminous Steel City Holdings LLC, that 10% is baked into your business model permanently. Deals that only cash flow if you self-manage are not your deals.
✅ PM + HCV = POWERFUL COMBO IN CRAFTON
A PM company experienced with Housing Choice Vouchers can significantly improve your income stability. HCV tenants in Crafton pay $1,100–$1,600/mo for 2–3BR units — often above market. Direct deposit from housing authority. Lower effective vacancy. Ask any PM you interview about their HCV experience specifically.
