⚙️ HOW ALLEGHENY COUNTY ASSESSMENT WORKS — CRITICAL TO UNDERSTAND
Base year is 2012. The Common Level Ratio (CLR) is 52.7% for 2025 — your property is assessed at ~52.7% of market value, not the purchase price. This means your tax bill is significantly lower than you'd expect at full value. A $200K purchase has taxes calculated on ~$105,400. Total combined millage = 43.5382 mills.

⚠️ THE PM REALITY CHECK — READ THIS BEFORE EVERY OFFER
A PM company at 10% of collected rents is a permanent expense that must be included in every scenario. This isn't optional for you — it's a business model decision. Deals that "work" for a self-managing investor often don't pencil for a PM-managed investor at the same price. Always run the PM-adjusted numbers.

Property Management — Crafton / Pittsburgh Guide
✅ SELF-MANAGE: VIABLE
Crafton's working-professional and commuter tenant base is manageable for a local self-manager. ~47% renter concentration is balanced — not so high that you're facing constant turnover. Crafton has the strongest rent growth in the playbook set (+2.6% YoY), so re-leasing events tend to be at improving rates. Self-managing is a real option if you're in the west corridor. Consider a leasing-only PM arrangement for tenant placement to capture the most valuable PM service without paying 10% monthly forever.
🏢 PM: SOLID COVERAGE IN THE WEST CORRIDOR
PM firms active in 15205 know the stock, the tenant pool, and the west corridor rental market. Full PM is the right answer if you're not local or are scaling to multiple west-side properties. At Crafton's price range ($100K–$300K), the 10% fee is workable — just model it before assuming it's optional.

💡 PM FEE UNDERWRITING — MODEL IT AS A PERMANENT EXPENSE
Never underwrite a deal assuming you'll eventually self-manage. If you're building a PM-managed portfolio under Luminous Steel City Holdings LLC, that 10% is baked into your business model permanently. Deals that only cash flow if you self-manage are not your deals.
✅ PM + HCV = POWERFUL COMBO IN CRAFTON
A PM company experienced with Housing Choice Vouchers can significantly improve your income stability. HCV tenants in Crafton pay $1,100–$1,600/mo for 2–3BR units — often above market. Direct deposit from housing authority. Lower effective vacancy. Ask any PM you interview about their HCV experience specifically.
