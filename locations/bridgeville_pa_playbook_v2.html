BRIDGEVILLE IS IN CHARTIERS VALLEY SD — CONFIRMED 34.87 COMBINED MILLS
Bridgeville Borough is served by Chartiers Valley School District (Bridgeville, Collier, Heidelberg, Scott) at 20.1909 mills — one of the lighter school levies in Allegheny County. Combined with the borough (8.25) and county (6.43), the total confirmed rate is 34.8709 mills — well below the Carlynton-SD neighbors Carnegie (43.44) and Crafton (43.54). That low rate is one of Bridgeville's best structural features. The calculator uses the 2026 CLR of 50.14% × 34.8709 mills. Pull the actual parcel tax bill before every offer.

Property Management — Bridgeville / South Hills Guide
✅ SELF-MANAGE: STRONGLY VIABLE
Bridgeville is one of the strongest self-manage candidates in the playbook. Working-professional and commuter tenants, a stable owner-heavy borough, low turnover, and the lowest-tier mill rate (34.87 mills) mean you're not fighting the tape on every cost line. Older stock but not extreme city-rowhouse aging. If you're local to the South Hills / west corridor and can commit to basic availability, self-managing a Bridgeville duplex or triplex is straightforward. Best option: a leasing-only PM arrangement — pay one placement fee for tenant screening, then self-manage the ongoing relationship.
🏢 PM: GOOD OPTION IF SCALING OR REMOTE
Full PM is a solid choice if you own multiple properties in the South Hills / Chartiers Valley corridor or aren't reliably within 30 minutes. PM firms active in 15017 have established vendor networks and leasing pipelines. At Bridgeville's moderate margins, the 10% fee is manageable — but with a thinner rental pool, ask specifically about days-to-lease. The leasing-only arrangement captures the most valuable PM service (screening and placement) without the ongoing monthly cost.
