Property Management — Carnegie / West Pittsburgh Guide
✅ SELF-MANAGE: STRONGLY VIABLE
Carnegie is the second-strongest self-manage candidate in the playbook. Working professional tenants, a stable borough, low turnover, and the second-lowest mill rate in the set (30.73) means you're not fighting the tape on every cost line. Older stock but not extreme city-rowhouse aging. If you're local to the west corridor and can commit to basic availability, self-managing a Carnegie duplex or triplex is straightforward. Best option: a leasing-only PM arrangement — pay one placement fee for tenant screening, then self-manage the ongoing relationship.
🏢 PM: GOOD OPTION IF SCALING OR REMOTE
Full PM is a solid choice if you own multiple properties in the west corridor or aren't reliably within 30 minutes. PM firms active in 15106 have established vendor networks and leasing pipelines. At Carnegie's moderate margins, the 10% fee is manageable — just not free. The leasing-only arrangement captures the most valuable PM service (screening and placement) without the ongoing monthly cost.
