💡 TAX APPEAL — USE THIS AGGRESSIVELY
If you acquire at or below market and the parcel assessment is stale (frequent in Bellevue's older stock), appeal immediately. File with BPAAR by September 1 each year for the following tax year. Target post-appeal assessed value = purchase price × 50.14%. With a 42.18-mill combined rate, a successful appeal on a $140K property can save $500–$900/yr — that's meaningful on a $2,100/mo duplex. Contingency-fee tax appeal firms operate throughout Allegheny County.

Property Management — Bellevue / North Pittsburgh Guide
⚠️ SELF-MANAGE: VIABLE WITH CONDITIONS
Bellevue's majority-renter profile (~55%) means more lease events per year than owner-occupant-heavy suburbs — you'll be re-leasing more often than in Bethel Park or Carnegie. Self-managing is viable if you're local to the north corridor and have bandwidth for more frequent tenant transitions. Two conditions apply: (1) have a reliable local contractor who can turn units quickly, and (2) even if you self-manage day-to-day, use a PM for tenant placement — screening quality at this tenant profile matters more than in lower-turnover markets.
✅ PM: EARNS ITS FEE HERE
This is one of the markets where PM delivers consistent value relative to cost. High renter concentration means frequent leasing activity — a PM's applicant pipeline, screening process, and faster days-to-lease directly reduces vacancy loss. At $1,195 average 2BR rents, the monthly PM fee (~$107/mo on a duplex EGI) is reasonable given the turnover frequency. Confirm active north shore / Bellevue corridor units and ask specifically about days-to-lease performance in 15202.

✅ PM + HCV = INCOME STABILITY IN A RENTER-HEAVY MARKET
Bellevue's ~55% renter share means a PM with ACHA Housing Choice Voucher experience is a real edge. ACHA payment standards for 2BR units in 15202 can reach $1,100–$1,350/mo — at or above the conservative underwrite. Direct deposit from the housing authority. Lower effective vacancy. Ask every PM you interview about their ACHA experience and days-to-inspection timeline specifically.
