Purchase Price Guide - PM-Adjusted Brighton Heights Screen
MODEL BASIS
Scenarios assume 20% down, 7.75% interest, 10% PM of EGI, 8% vacancy, 10% repairs, 3% other expenses, $1,500 annual insurance, 27.307-mill CLR-modeled taxes, and duplex gross rent of $2,400/mo unless stated otherwise.
TIER 1 - STRONG DUPLEX SCREEN
$100,000 - $180,000
STRONG OUTPUT IF CONDITION AND LEGAL USE VERIFY
At $180K, the modeled duplex produces about $313/mo cash flow, an 8.97% cap rate, 7.88% cash-on-cash return, and 1.30x DSCR. A purchase materially below the neighborhood median warrants close inspection and legal-unit verification.
TIER 2 - VERIFY OR REPRICE
$180,000 - $200,000
MARGIN THINS QUICKLY
At $185K the model returns about $279/mo and 1.26x DSCR but misses the 7% cash-on-cash hurdle. At $200K it produces about $176/mo and 1.15x DSCR, below the acquisition thresholds.
TIER 3 - SEEK THREE UNITS
$200,000 - $260,000
DUPLEX WEAK; TRIPLEX MAY PENCIL
A modeled triplex with $3,225 monthly gross rent reaches about $408/mo, 8.84% cap, 7.39% cash-on-cash return, and 1.28x DSCR at $250K. Require legal three-unit status and verified rents.
TIER 4 - HIGH DILIGENCE
$260,000 - $300,000
NO STANDARD DUPLEX ACQUISITION
The conservative duplex case no longer pencils. A triplex at $300K yields only about $64/mo and 1.04x DSCR; a four-unit candidate requires verified income, legal use, condition, and comparable support before consideration.
