Property Management - Brighton Heights / Pittsburgh Guide
⚠️ SELF-MANAGE: VIABLE WITH CONDITIONS
Brighton Heights is City of Pittsburgh stock — older buildings, city permit requirements, and more variable maintenance complexity than suburban markets. Self-managing is viable for local investors comfortable with city multifamily operations, but the older stock generates more maintenance surprises than Carnegie or Bethel Park. Two things to have in place before self-managing: (1) a reliable city-familiar contractor network, and (2) familiarity with Pittsburgh city rental registration and occupancy requirements. The low city mill rate (27.31) means the PM fee isn't as painful as Brentwood, but it still bites at these margins.
✅ PM: CITY EXPERTISE MATTERS HERE
A PM with active Pittsburgh city multifamily experience navigates code compliance, permit history issues, and city rental registration more efficiently than most self-managers. Brighton Heights has strong rent growth (+3.6% YoY) — a PM who knows the local market can capture that at re-lease time. Good choice if you're not north-side local or if city multifamily operations are new to you.
