⚠️ BRENTWOOD SD: 39.20 MILLS — THE HIGHEST SD RATE IN THE PLAYBOOK SET

Property Management — Brentwood / South Pittsburgh Guide
⚠️ SELF-MANAGE: VIABLE BELOW $120K — RISKY ABOVE $130K
At 56.63 mills, the PM fee is often the difference between a deal that pencils and one that doesn't at higher price points. Self-managing below $120K is genuinely viable — the wide margin at low acquisition prices absorbs the risk of a slow lease-up or a problem tenant. Above $130K the numbers are tighter, and a bad tenant, an eviction, or an extended vacancy costs more than 10–15 months of PM fees. The rule here: don't use self-management to rescue a marginal deal. If a deal only pencils without the PM fee, that's a red flag about the deal — not a reason to self-manage.
✅ PM: HIGHEST ROI DECISION IN THIS MARKET
At 56.63 mills, the PM fee has its highest dollar cost in the playbook — but also its highest value per dollar. Thin margins mean a bad tenant costs more here than anywhere else in the set. A PM with active ACHA/HCV experience in 15227, established vendor relationships, and a strong days-to-lease track record is worth the fee above $130K. Ask specifically: what is your average days-to-lease for a vacant 2BR in Brentwood? Any number above 30 days at 56 mills is a real cost.
