THE PM REALITY CHECK
Bethel Park's higher published rents improve revenue potential, but the market's higher prices and a smaller renter share leave little room for optimistic assumptions. Keep the PM fee, vacancy, repairs, and assessment verification in every offer model.
Purchase Price Tier Guide — PM-Adjusted, Bethel Park 2–4 Units
MODEL BASIS
Screening cases assume 20% down, 7.75% interest, 10% PM of EGI, 8% vacancy, 10% repairs, 3% other expenses, $1,500 annual insurance, CLR-modeled taxes, and duplex GPR of $2,700/mo.
TIER 1 — EXCEPTIONAL SCREEN
$100,000 – $185,000
🟢 STRONG MODEL OUTPUT IF CONDITION IS REAL
A duplex at $185K generates about $406/mo cash flow, 9.51% cap rate, and 1.38× DSCR in the base model. Because this range is well below the broader Bethel Park sales market, verify unit legality, systems, leases, and deferred maintenance before relying on the output.
TIER 2 — DUPLEX TARGET RANGE
$185,000 – $198,000
🟡 BUY IF RENTS AND TAXES VERIFY
A $195K screening case generates approximately $333/mo, 8.93% cap rate, and 1.30× DSCR. At approximately $198K the modeled duplex is near the minimum cash-flow and CoC thresholds. Any rent concession, utility burden, or higher actual tax bill can erase the margin.
TIER 3 — THREE-UNIT PREFERENCE
$198,000 – $260,000
🟡 DUPLEX MARGIN THINS — SEEK 3+ UNITS
At $200K the modeled duplex is marginal at about $297/mo cash flow, 8.66% cap rate, and 1.26× DSCR because it misses the CoC and per-unit cash-flow targets. At $240K it falls to about $4/mo cash flow and 1.00× DSCR, and at $260K it turns negative.
TIER 4 — HIGH DILIGENCE
$260,000 – $300,000
🔴 NO STANDARD DUPLEX ACQUISITION
A $300K duplex is materially negative under the conservative rent model. A triplex at the modeled $3,775 GPR reaches approximately 8.14% cap and 1.18× DSCR at $300K, remaining just below the DSCR threshold unless verified income or price improves.
