Property Management — Bethel Park / South Hills Guide
✅ SELF-MANAGE: VIABLE
Bethel Park's tenant profile — families, transit commuters, professionals — is stable and manageable. Low renter concentration (~24%) means fewer lease events per year and a self-selecting pool of qualified applicants. If you're south of Pittsburgh and accessible within 30–40 minutes, self-managing is a real option. Best middle path: a leasing-only PM arrangement for tenant placement (one-time fee for screening), then self-manage the ongoing relationship. Model the full 10% PM fee in your long-term hold regardless of your near-term plan.
🏢 PM: MAKES SENSE AT SCALE
Full PM becomes the right answer once you own 2–3 properties in the south hills corridor — coordination overhead adds up faster than the monthly fee feels painful. PM firms covering Bethel Park, Mt. Lebanon, and Baldwin have strong coverage here. At a $306K median price point the 10% fee is the most expensive in the south suburbs — model it carefully before treating it as a default.
